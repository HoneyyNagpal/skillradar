    Where a new skill pays off first

    A transparent heuristic, not a forecast: half entry-level demand (overall demand while there are too few entry-level postings to measure), a quarter recent growth, a quarter breadth across roles (number of role families where the skill appears in at least 10% of postings). Each component is shown so you can disagree with the weights.

    Method and limits

    Postings are collected weekly, kept raw, then cleaned, deduplicated (same title, company and city counts once) and tagged with skills by a dictionary matcher. Trends use the week a posting was first seen and a Cochran-Armitage test for a linear trend in proportions. Trend, pair and city findings are corrected for multiple testing with Benjamini-Hochberg at 5%. Skills below the minimum sample sizes are not reported.

    Known limits: skill extraction is a dictionary, so it misses skills named in unusual ways; job APIs often return truncated descriptions; most postings carry no salary, so pay figures describe a self-selected minority; the planner measures fit to what postings list, not interview outcomes.
